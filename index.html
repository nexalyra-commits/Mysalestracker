About This App

📦 About

My Sales Tracker helps you manage customers, orders, and follow-ups — all in one place. Track every order from creation to delivery.

💾 Offline Storage

This app works fully offline. All your data is stored locally on your device.

⚠️ If the app is uninstalled or app data is cleared, all data will be permanently lost. There is no cloud backup.

🚀 How to Use

  • Tap "Add Customer" to create a new order record.
  • Use the search bar to find customers or products quickly.
  • Use status filter chips to view orders by status.
  • Tap a card to view full order details.
  • Tap the arrow button on a card to expand or collapse it.
  • Use Edit to update an order, Delete to remove it.
  • Use the social buttons to contact a customer directly.

⚪ Button Guide

  • ➕ Add Customer — Create a new order record.
  • ✏️ Edit — Update an existing order.
  • 🗑️ Delete — Permanently remove an order.
  • − All / + All — Collapse or expand all cards at once.
  • 📱 Social icons — Contact the customer via their platform.
  • ❓ Help icon — Opens this guide.

💬 Auto Messages

Messages sent to customers are automatically tailored to their order status (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled, On-Hold) and include their name and product details.
